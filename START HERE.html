Your new website: start here

Your website has two parts: the public site where clients see your services and book online, and a private dashboard where you manage bookings, prices, hours, and everything else. No technical skills needed.

Put your website online (about 10 minutes)

Your website runs on Render, a company that keeps it online 24/7. Their smallest plan with safe storage for your bookings costs a few dollars a month (about $7; check render.com for current prices). There's no booking fee on top.

  1. Click this button

    Deploy to Render →

    It opens Render in your browser.

  2. Sign up or sign in to Render

    Create a free account (you can use your Google account). Add a payment card when asked.

  3. Choose your dashboard password

    Render shows a box named ADMIN_PASSWORD. Type the password you want to use for your dashboard (at least 8 characters), then click Deploy Blueprint (it may say Apply).

  4. Wait for "Live"

    Setup takes about 5 minutes. When your website shows Live, click its link at the top. It looks like tidosity-website.onrender.com.

  5. Open your dashboard

    Add /admin to the end of your website's link, sign in with your password, and follow the short checklist: phone and email, hours, prices, and email alerts.

Everyday use

Bookmark your dashboard (yourwebsite/admin). Everything is changed there, and your website updates instantly.

Bookings

See upcoming jobs with the address (tap for directions), phone, and notes. Mark jobs done or cancel.

Schedule

Set your working hours and travel time between jobs, and block off vacations or days off.

Services & prices

Change prices, add or hide services, and upload photos straight from your phone.

Business info

Phone, email, the towns you serve, reviews, and your FAQ.

Email alerts

Get an email for every booking. Clients get confirmations automatically.

Password

Change your dashboard password anytime.

Use your own web address (optional)

  1. In Render

    Open your website, go to Settings, then Custom Domains, and add your address (for example tidosity.com). Render shows one or two records to copy.

  2. Where you bought your domain

    Open its DNS settings (for a Squarespace domain: Squarespace → Domains → DNS) and add the records Render showed you. It can take a few hours to start working.

  3. Back in your dashboard

    Under Business info, update Your website's address so links in emails use your new address.

Questions

I forgot my dashboard password

In Render, open your website and go to Environment. Change ADMIN_PASSWORD to a new password and click Save Changes. The site restarts in about a minute, and the new password works.

Can I try the website on my computer first?

Windows: double-click Start Website (Windows). Mac: right-click Start Website (Mac) and choose Open. If your computer asks you to install Node.js, install the "LTS" version and try again. Your dashboard opens in your browser; close the black window to stop.

This is only a preview on your computer. Clients can't see it, and bookings made there stay on your computer. Your real website is the one on Render.

How do I keep a copy of my bookings?

In your dashboard, open Bookings and click Download all (spreadsheet). It opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

Will I lose bookings if the website restarts or updates?

No. The Deploy button sets up a storage disk on Render that keeps your bookings and settings safe through restarts and updates.
