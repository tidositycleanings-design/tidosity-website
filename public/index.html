About Tidosity

A woman-owned business built on peace of mind

Founded in 2024 by Sabrina Ureta, Tidosity Premium Cleaning Services began with residential cleaning and grew quickly through referrals from friends, family, and clients who trusted Sabrina's attention to detail, reliability, and commitment to quality.

In 2026, Tidosity expanded to specialize in short-term rental turnovers. Sabrina's background as a Project Coordinator for a property management company, and later as an on-site Airbnb turnover specialist, shaped systems built for efficiency, consistency, and exceptional guest readiness.

Today we proudly serve homeowners who want a spotless, stress-free home and hosts who depend on reliable turnovers to deliver five-star guest experiences.

  • Detail-oriented. Meticulous cleaning, from top to bottom.
  • Dependable. Consistent service you can plan around.
  • Hospitality driven. Damage documentation, inventory, and restocking for hosts.

Our mission

To create clean, comfortable, and welcoming spaces while giving our clients more time and peace of mind.
